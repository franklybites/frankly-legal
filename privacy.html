Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Frankly (“Frankly,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Frankly app and related services.

Information you provide

  • Account information: identifiers and email address used to register, sign in, and manage your account.
  • Profile information: username, display name, avatar URL, and bio if you choose to provide them.
  • Reviews and photos: ratings, written reviews, restaurant or hot dog information you submit, and photos you upload.
  • Support communications: information you include when you contact support.

Location and nearby restaurant search

If you grant location permission, Frankly uses your device location to find nearby restaurants and return relevant results. Location is used for this search and is not saved as a location history or stored as your GPS coordinates in your Frankly profile or app database. Your device may send coordinates to Frankly's service to fulfill the search request. You can deny or revoke location permission in your device settings; nearby results may then be limited or unavailable. You can still use parts of the app that do not require location.

Service providers

Frankly uses Supabase for account authentication, database services, and photo storage. Frankly uses Google Places-related services to find or display restaurant and place information. These providers process information as needed to deliver their services and are subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

How we use information

  • To create and manage accounts and profiles.
  • To display reviews, ratings, photos, restaurant information, and rankings.
  • To find nearby restaurants when you use location-based search.
  • To maintain service functionality, protect against abuse, troubleshoot problems, and respond to support requests.

Content visibility

Information and content submitted to community features—such as profile names, reviews, ratings, and photos—may be visible to other app users. Do not submit content you do not want other users to see. Account details used for authentication are not intended to be displayed as public profile content.

Sharing

We use service providers described above to operate Frankly. Content you submit may be shown to other users as part of the service. We may also disclose information when required by law, to address security or abuse, or to protect rights and safety. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and account deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations. You can initiate account deletion from the Delete Account option in your in-app Profile. Deletion is intended to remove your account and associated personal content, including your reviews and uploaded photos. Shared restaurant and hot dog listings may remain available to the community. Some records may be retained where legally required or necessary for legitimate security and legal purposes.

Security

We use access controls and security features provided by our service infrastructure to help protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

Frankly is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

Your choices and contact

You can manage location permission through your device settings, edit information available in your profile, and initiate account deletion in the app. For privacy questions or requests concerning personal information, email franklybites.support@gmail.com.
