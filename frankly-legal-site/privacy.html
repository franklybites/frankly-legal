Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Frankly (“Frankly,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Frankly app and related services. Review this draft carefully and update it to reflect the app's actual configuration before publication.

Information you provide

  • Account information: email address and account identifiers used for registration and sign-in.
  • Profile information: username, display name, avatar URL, and bio if you choose to provide them.
  • User content: restaurant and hot dog ratings, review text, and photos you submit.
  • Support messages: information you choose to include when contacting support.

Location and restaurant search

If you grant location permission, Frankly may use your device's location to search for nearby restaurants. You can deny or revoke location permission in your device settings; some nearby-search features may then be unavailable. Verify whether precise coordinates are stored or only sent to a search service, and update this section to match the implementation.

Service providers

Frankly uses Supabase for authentication, database services, and photo storage. Frankly also uses Google Places services to find or display place information. Those providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies. Confirm the exact APIs and configuration used by the production app before launch.

How information is used

  • To operate accounts, profiles, reviews, rankings, and photo uploads.
  • To find and display nearby restaurants and place details.
  • To prevent abuse, enforce app rules, troubleshoot errors, and maintain security.
  • To respond to support and privacy requests.

Public content

Restaurant reviews, ratings, usernames or display names, and submitted photos may be visible to other users depending on the app's current settings. Do not submit information or photos you do not want to share publicly. Confirm the app's profile and photo visibility settings before launch.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information as a business practice described by this draft. Information may be processed by the service providers listed above, when you direct us to share content, or when disclosure is required to comply with law or protect the service. Revise this section if your app uses analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or additional integrations.

Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to operate the service and for legitimate security, dispute-resolution, or legal purposes. Users should be able to initiate account deletion from within the app. Account deletion should remove or de-identify associated personal data and user content where appropriate, subject to any legal retention requirements. Do not publish this statement until the deletion workflow is implemented and tested.

Security

We use access controls and service-provider security features intended to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

Frankly is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact support.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. The current version will be posted on this page. For privacy questions or requests, contact franklybites.support@gmail.com.

Launch review: Confirm age requirements, location behavior, photo visibility, deletion behavior, and any analytics/advertising SDKs. Update this policy before publishing if any statement does not match the live app.
