Contact Support

Need help with Frankly? Contact us about account access, account deletion, technical problems, restaurant information corrections, privacy questions, or general feedback.

Frankly Support

franklybites.support@gmail.com

For community reports, use the subject line “Community Report.” For privacy requests, mention “Privacy Request” in your subject.

What to include

Describe the issue and include only the information needed to help us understand it. If your request concerns a restaurant, include its name and location. If you are reporting a review, include enough detail to identify the content.

Keep your account secure

Never send your password, authentication codes, or other sign-in secrets by email. If you cannot complete an in-app account deletion request, contact us and explain what happened.
