Community Guidelines

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Frankly works best when reviews help people decide where to eat. These guidelines apply to ratings, written reviews, profiles, and uploaded photos.

Do

  • Share honest opinions based on your own experience.
  • Keep reviews relevant to the restaurant or hot dog being reviewed.
  • Use photos that accurately represent the food or place.
  • Be specific and respectful when describing a good or bad experience.
  • Report content that appears fraudulent, abusive, or unsafe.

Don't

  • Post fake reviews, paid reviews presented as independent, or coordinated rating manipulation.
  • Upload photos you do not have permission to use.
  • Post threats, harassment, hate, sexual exploitation, or unlawful content.
  • Publish private information about customers, staff, or other users.
  • Spam, impersonate others, or use multiple accounts to distort rankings.

Reporting and moderation

Use the app's reporting feature when available, or contact support. Frankly may remove content or restrict accounts that violate these guidelines or the Terms of Service. Please include enough information for us to identify the content.

Contact

Questions or reports can be sent to franklybites.support@gmail.com.
