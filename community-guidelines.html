Community Guidelines

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Frankly helps people discover hot dogs and share their experiences. These guidelines apply to ratings, written reviews, profiles, restaurant information, and uploaded photos.

What we encourage

  • Share honest opinions based on your own experience.
  • Keep reviews relevant to the restaurant or hot dog being reviewed.
  • Use photos that accurately represent the food or location.
  • Be specific and respectful when describing your experience.
  • Correct mistakes and report content that appears fraudulent, abusive, or unsafe.

What is not allowed

  • Fake reviews, paid reviews presented as independent, or coordinated rating manipulation.
  • Photos or other content you do not have permission to use.
  • Threats, harassment, hateful abuse, or unlawful content.
  • Private personal information about customers, staff, business owners, or other users.
  • Spam, impersonation, deceptive activity, or multiple accounts used to distort rankings.

Reporting a concern

To report inappropriate reviews, offensive photos, harassment, or suspected rating manipulation, email franklybites.support@gmail.com with the subject line Community Report. Include the relevant restaurant or review details and explain the concern. Do not include passwords or sign-in codes.

Moderation

Frankly may review reports and remove content or restrict accounts that violate these guidelines or the Terms of Service. We may consider the nature of the report, available information, and the need to protect the community. Sending a report does not guarantee a particular outcome.

Questions

If you need help with a report or community issue, contact Frankly Support.
