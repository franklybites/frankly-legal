Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Frankly (“Frankly,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Frankly app and related services. Review this draft and ensure it matches the production app before publication.

Information you provide

  • Account information: email address and account identifiers used for registration and sign-in.
  • Profile information: username, display name, avatar URL, and bio if you choose to provide them.
  • User content: restaurant and hot dog ratings, review text, and photos you submit.
  • Support messages: information you include when contacting support.

Location and restaurant search

If you grant location permission, Frankly may use your device's location to search for nearby restaurants. You can deny or revoke location permission in device settings; nearby-search features may then be limited. Confirm whether coordinates are stored or sent to a search service and revise this section to match the implementation.

Service providers

Frankly uses Supabase for authentication, database services, and photo storage. Frankly also uses Google Places services to find or display place information. Confirm the exact APIs and configuration used by the production app before launch.

How information is used

  • To operate accounts, profiles, reviews, rankings, and photo uploads.
  • To find and display nearby restaurants and place details.
  • To prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, maintain security, and respond to support requests.

Public content

Restaurant reviews, ratings, profile names, and submitted photos may be visible to other users depending on the app's settings. Do not submit information or photos you do not want to share publicly. Confirm actual visibility settings before launch.

Sharing and retention

Information may be processed by the service providers listed above, shared when you direct us to share content, or disclosed when required by law. We retain information as needed to operate and secure the service and to address legal obligations. Update this section if the app uses analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or additional integrations.

Security and children

We use access controls and provider security features intended to protect information, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Frankly is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Privacy questions and requests

For privacy questions, requests concerning personal information, or account deletion assistance, contact Frankly Support at franklybites.support@gmail.com.

Before launch: Confirm location processing, photo visibility, retention, account deletion, age requirements, and all third-party SDKs. These statements must match the live app.
